Facility Overview

A key link in the New Gumma pumping chain

SJPNL documentation describes the New Gumma Water Treatment Plant as having a two-stage arrangement. Stage-I pumping carries treated water from New Gumma to Drabla; Stage-II pumping then carries water from Drabla to Craignano Reservoir.

From Craignano, the documented route proceeds toward Sanjauli and the wider Shimla system. This portal combines official utility information, Himachal Pradesh government documents, World Bank technical material and historical/academic research.

Stage-I New Gumma → Drabla
Stage-II Drabla → Craignano Reservoir
Supply route Craignano → Sanjauli / Shimla
Current pump status, current production, present-day operating hours and official departmental contact details should be updated from live departmental records before public release.

STAGE II INTERMEDIATE STATION

Drabla Pumping Station

Elevation: 1,450 meters MSL (Coordinates: 31.185°N, 77.228°E)

Receives treated bulk water from New Gumma (650m) via Stage-I rising main. Accommodates 4 dedicated high-head multistage centrifugal pump sets (Pumps 1, 2, 3 on bed and Pump 4 maintenance bay) powered by 33kV dedicated feeders, lifting water an additional 800m static head toward Craignano ridge.

Rising Main: 450mm dia Mild Steel
Design Lift: +800 Meters (+1,006m Dynamic)
Installed Capacity: 24.06 MLD Bulk Supply
Commissioning: 1981–82 Augmentation
