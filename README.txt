 GUMMA / DRABLA PUMPING STATION WEBSITE
===========================================

Project:
IPH Department - Drabla Gumma
LWSS Gumma - Bulk Water Supply Scheme
Drabla Pumping Station - Second Stage Pumping, New Gumma Line

Overview
--------
This folder contains a complete static website for a professional government
water-infrastructure information portal documenting Gumma / Drabla water-supply
history, the New Gumma double-stage pumping route, machinery references,
source documentation and supplied facility photographs.

Core Sections
-------------
1. Sticky header with top utility bar and responsive hamburger navigation.
2. Hero section using the Drabla Pumping Station exterior photograph.
3. Key facts cards for 1921-22, 1981-82, 24.06 MLD documented design capacity
   and the New Gumma two-stage route.
4. About section explaining Stage-I: New Gumma -> Drabla and Stage-II:
   Drabla -> Craignano Reservoir.
5. Dark-blue water-system section with highlighted Drabla flow diagram and
   separate Nauti Khad WTP -> Mashobra -> Sanjauli route card.
6. Engineering and machinery section with cards for pumping sets, electrical
   control, rising mains/valves and maintenance categories.
7. Technical reference table with documented audit-period figures.
8. Old Gumma historical section and 1921-22 badge.
9. Historical timeline from 1875 to 2024-26.
10. 24x7 development section distinguishing modern utility projects from
    historical Gumma/Drabla infrastructure.
11. Three-image supplied gallery with fullscreen lightbox.
12. Research source cards and cautious contact-reference section.
13. Footer with quick links and publication checklist.

Accuracy Notes
--------------
The website intentionally avoids fabricated live production values, current
operating status, employee names, direct Drabla office contacts and official
logo claims. Technical values are labelled as documented audit/project-period
figures. Current plant specifications, operating hours, production and official
department contact details should be verified from live departmental records
before public release.

Viewing
-------
